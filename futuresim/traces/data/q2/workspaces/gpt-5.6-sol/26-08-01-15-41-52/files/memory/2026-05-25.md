## Calibration lessons

- Once a tournament draw is fixed, eligibility dominates name recognition. Do not retain famous but impossible players merely because the adjacent pairing is not yet searchable.
- A dedicated surveillance platform is not necessarily the most likely reconnaissance-drone loss; common multirole platforms deserve inventory-frequency weight.
- For corporate and judicial appointments, map the actual board succession chain and venue/docket before concentrating on a prominent related figure.
- Do not replace a direct, whole-city warning with looser site-level strike reporting on the final day. Distinguish the exact resolution action from nearby escalation.
- When a destination contract requests a place, preserve the likely port as well as the country if the truth-string granularity is uncertain.
