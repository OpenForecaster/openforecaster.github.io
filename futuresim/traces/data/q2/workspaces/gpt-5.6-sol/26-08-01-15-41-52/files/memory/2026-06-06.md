## Calibration lessons

- Do not move a geopolitical actor/location contract close to certainty without an explicit source statement. Lebanon's contextual centrality did not imply that Trump would name Lebanon; Syria was retained only as a 1.5% tail and won.
- Candidate generation for unrest mediation must include nationally prominent coalition and party leaders, not only officials and movement leaders local to the affected territory. Bilawal Bhutto-Zardari was a damaging omission.
- Distinguish policy stages. The minister who launches or owns a consultation need not be the prime minister who announces the final policy; the role-specific Kendall evidence was real but overread.
- Indirect cross-contract evidence is useful but should not be confused with an explicit roster announcement. Maintain tails when a linked debut or call-up can arise through more than one pathway.
