## Calibration lessons

- Read resolution criteria literally when a title is leading or grammatically presupposes an upset. The winner of Sinner's match can be Sinner.
- Exact outcome strings matter. If the requested object is a model designation, prefer `GlobalEye` over manufacturer-prefixed `Saab GlobalEye` unless the expected naming convention is explicit.
- Do not turn a weak geographic narrative into 93% when several politically salient borders remain plausible. The Pakistan miss was the day's largest avoidable overconfidence error.
- A player headlining a squad is not evidence that he is captain. Prefer direct role language and prior armband history.
- Once a final squad or completed draw leg is official, immediately remove ineligible players; roster eligibility dominates prior scoring priors.
- Translate close polls into event probabilities rather than copying the nominal leader with near certainty. A 1.4-point lead with substantial polling disagreement supports a moderate favourite, not 95%.
