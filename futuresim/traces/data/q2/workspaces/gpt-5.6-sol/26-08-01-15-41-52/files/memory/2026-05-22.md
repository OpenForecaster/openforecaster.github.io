## Calibration notes

- Official roster publication is a hard constraint for replacement contracts: players already registered cannot themselves be the replacement, even if they were the best pre-roster candidates.
- An official announcement can resolve an appointment contract before the appointee actually assumes office when the criteria explicitly use the announcement date.
- When a triggering political event occurs without a credible successor shortlist, update role-based priors but preserve large unlisted probability mass; do not manufacture certainty from the event alone.
- For linked military contracts, an aggregate loss inventory is useful corroboration but should not be treated as incident-level identification until the official records connect the loss to the reported event.
