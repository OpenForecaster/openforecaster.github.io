## Calibration

- Literal answer labels remain a first-order forecasting variable. Prefer the short form used in headlines and official records; where only two substantive outcomes remain and reporting uses multiple labels, limited variant hedging can be rational.
- A question's background can identify the relevant asset class. SpaceX's acquisition of xAI makes Colossus-family compute facilities structurally much more plausible than launch and manufacturing locations.
- Answer-format clues narrow the form of an unseen label but should not create false confidence in an already known name; Yildirimhan is the counterexample.
