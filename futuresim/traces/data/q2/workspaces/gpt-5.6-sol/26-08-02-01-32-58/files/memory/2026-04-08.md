## Calibration lessons

- When an official or near-official report names the exact actor, propagate that evidence to related future-role markets instead of retaining institutionally plausible incumbents.
- For elections, use current head-to-head polling and seat projections rather than stale candidate prominence. Preserve uncertainty when multiple credible pollsters disagree.
- The three new resolutions again show that an unconfirmed analogue should rarely receive heavy concentration when several specific candidates remain viable.
