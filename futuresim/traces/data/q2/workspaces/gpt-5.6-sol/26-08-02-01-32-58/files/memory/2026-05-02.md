## Calibration

- A question's grammar can impose a hard candidate constraint. Apply it before assigning prominence-based probabilities.
- Replace pre-tournament priors immediately when finalists are known; eliminated teams should have zero probability.
- For near-clinched leagues, calculate the remaining-points path rather than relying on table rank alone.
- Distinguish internal operation/coalition designations from the public name of the event or project the question actually resolves on.
- When a future question asks about a named pollster, the pollster's immediately preceding survey is much stronger evidence than general polling averages.
