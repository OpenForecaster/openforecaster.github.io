## Calibration lessons

- A multi-round contest with a two-point lead and only four rounds left can justify very high confidence even when the original favourite was elsewhere; remaining-game arithmetic matters more than priors.
- In fragmented elections, preserve meaningful probability for every candidate inside the polling margin of error. The Djibouti miss and Peru's three-way technical tie both reinforce the cost of omitting plausible named alternatives.
- Live sports evidence is valuable but time-sensitive. When only part of the field has finished, raise a completed leader without collapsing uncertainty around players still on the course.
