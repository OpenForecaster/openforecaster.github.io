## Calibration reminders

- Match the exact causal clause in the question. A highly salient adjacent event is not decisive if it does not satisfy the specified mechanism.
- Institutional titles can mislead: the official who leads an administrative unit may differ from the person overseeing the named political plan.
- Ceremonial protocol is less deterministic than assumed; retain meaningful probability for a senior regional figure even when an award is municipal.
- Direct quotations and live counts justify aggressive movement, but preserve uncertainty for exact labels and geographically non-random outstanding votes.
