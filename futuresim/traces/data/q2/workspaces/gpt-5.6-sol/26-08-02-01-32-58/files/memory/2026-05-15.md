## Calibration lessons

- Re-anchor geographic forecasts immediately when a new outbreak or disaster identifies a different operating region. Locations from previous incidents are actively misleading once the current epicentre is known.
- Functional office mapping matters more than a story's diplomatic or legal label. Mohsin Naqvi's mediation role shows that the responsible security official can be the named actor even when foreign-ministry figures appear more obvious.
- Preserve upset mass in contests with broad fields and limited current evidence. Bulgaria's Eurovision win and Xabi Alonso's Chelsea appointment both came from candidates that were covered only lightly or omitted from the top slate.
