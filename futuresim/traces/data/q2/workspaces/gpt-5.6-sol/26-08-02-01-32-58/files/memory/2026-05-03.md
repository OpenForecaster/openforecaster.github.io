## Calibration

- When standings arithmetic makes all but two outcomes impossible, collapse the slate immediately rather than preserving pre-event tail mass.
- A directly observed pace convergence should affect later race markets even when the incumbent team still wins; distinguish result dominance from underlying performance dominance.
- Exact answer-format constraints are evidence. Remove multiword or already-unveiled alternatives when a question explicitly requires a new one-word prototype name.
- Do not treat a high-quality polling outlier as negligible solely because it conflicts with the modal polling story; Tamil Nadu shows that a meaningful tail should remain on the divergent scenario.
