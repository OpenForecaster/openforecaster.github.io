## Lessons

- Exact historical analogues can be badly misleading for highly specific future incidents: the Peshawar and Quetta gate-attack analogues obscured Karachi. Keep more mass on large-base-rate cities when the event has not occurred.
- Recent player-level event patterns are useful but should not crowd out defenders and substitutes in an exact match incident; Davinson Sanchez was not covered.
- Once an event's candidate roster becomes broad and concrete, assign pair probabilities from the full combinatorial space. Do not let a few salient bilateral pairings absorb most of the mass.
- In random-draw questions, each named candidate should stay close to the draw base rate until the actual opponent is observable; remove seeded or unavailable players promptly.
