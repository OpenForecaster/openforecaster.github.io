## Calibration reminders

- Product form matters for brand forecasts: jars, pouches, formula, and cereal identify different competitive sets.
- For local incidents, largest-city priors can be actively misleading. Allocate outcomes to plausible small municipalities when the mechanism is rural or site-specific.
- Adjacent evidence can improve candidate generation even when it cannot confirm the answer. Separate candidate inclusion from probability concentration.
- Read background prose for the event it is implicitly anchoring; generic geography should not override a specific operational context.
