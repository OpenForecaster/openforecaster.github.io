## Calibration Lessons
- If an article lists both a municipality and smaller towns, include the municipality when the market asks for a "town" unless the criterion explicitly excludes it.
- A direct poll lead near election day can outweigh incumbent advantage, but keep meaningful incumbent mass where electoral institutions are contested.
- When an exact phrase appears in a later question's title, even if the article date differs, treat it as a strong location clue.
