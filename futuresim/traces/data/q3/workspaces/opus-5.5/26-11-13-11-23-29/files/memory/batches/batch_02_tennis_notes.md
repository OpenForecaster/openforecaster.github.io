- **Alcaraz**: right-wrist tenosynovitis at Barcelona (Apr 14). Missed Madrid, Rome, RG, Queen's and Wimbledon (withdrew May 19). As of June 18-20 he was doing racket work and left-handed hitting only, with no forehands yet; an MRI/medical review with Dr. Ruiz-Cotorro was pending around June 20-21. RNE (June 15) reported the comeback options as Los Cabos (ATP 250, Jul 27), Washington (ATP 500, Jul 27) or Montreal (Masters, Aug 2-13); La Verdad said the focus is on "August". No return date has been announced. He defends the Cincinnati title (1000) and the US Open title (2000).

Tests at J Medical and San Raffaele (cardiac MRI and others) found no pathology, so it was treated as fatigue. He played no grass warm-ups and beat Norrie 6-3 6-3 at the Hurlingham exhibition on June 24. He opens Wimbledon June 29.
