## 408396 Census 2030 status
- Trump's Aug 2025 post said people here illegally "WILL NOT BE COUNTED". Lutnick has been ambiguous.
- Missouri v. Commerce (E.D. Mo. 4:26-cv-00131, filed Jan 30 2026, AG Hanaway) seeks to exclude "illegal aliens and temporary visa holders". That leaves citizens plus legal permanent residents. DOJ defending; three-judge panel denied Apr 3, renewed motion briefed May 2026.
- Earlier Louisiana-led suit sought the same exclusion. The 2020 Trump memo excluded only people "not in a lawful immigration status".
- The 2026 Census Test uses the ACS form, which has a citizenship question.
