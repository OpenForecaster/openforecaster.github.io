## 683823 / 772343 Nuclear drought shutdown -> Romania
- Danube at Baziaș 2,600-3,600 m3/s in June 2026, against a June mean of 5,900. INHGA forecast 2,700 for 21-28 Jun and expects the June regime at 30-50% of normal. Calafat flow was below 2,200 (capital.ro 21 Jun; gandul 9 Jun).
- Greenpeace RO (20 May 2026) warned that cooling water for the Cernavoda reactor could be interrupted.
- Cernavoda procedure (SNN 2015): orderly shutdown of units at set aspiration-basin levels. In 2011 the threshold was about 2.35 m for the second unit. In 2003 Unit 1 was shut on 23-24 Aug at a flow of 1,620 m3/s.
- Both Cernavoda units were offline in May 2026 (U2 transformer failure on 4 May; U1 planned outage from 10 May), with restarts expected in June. U1 refurbishment starts in 2027.
