## Bahrain replacement (918398)
- Mar 14: F1 said Bahrain and Saudi "will not take place in April", with no substitutes. Imola and Portimao were considered for April but rejected.
- May: the realistic options are:
  - reinstate Bahrain (FIA's preference, freight still in the country) or Saudi (pushing hard) on Oct 2-4, between Baku and Singapore;
  - an extra race at season end, with the finale moved to Dec 13;
  - Turkey/Istanbul Park brought forward from 2027 (5-year deal 2027-31), per Ben Sulayem "if it finishes its homologation".
- Imola's mayor says the circuit is "ready". Domenicali (May 27) has a contingency plan for Qatar/Abu Dhabi. A decision on Turkey is "not expected before the summer".
- No June articles found on any decision.
- Jun 24 context: a US-Iran deal has been reached and the Hormuz crisis is easing, which raises the chance of reinstating Bahrain itself.
- Big risk: no announcement at all (not representable in the answer set).
