# Batch 05 - Transfers B: evidence notes (sim date 2026-06-24)

Searches used: 31 of 42 cap. The local articles file (Jun 23-24) had almost no transfer coverage.

## Context (summer 2026)
- PL window opened Jun 15 and closes Sep 1. Brazil's international window opens Jul 20.
- New coaches: Man City - Enzo Maresca (Guardiola gone); Chelsea - Xabi Alonso (from Jul 1; Chelsea have no European football); Liverpool - Andoni Iraola (Slot sacked); Real Madrid - Jose Mourinho (Perez re-elected); Man Utd - Michael Carrick; Spurs - Roberto De Zerbi; Juventus - Spalletti (CEO Comolli / Carnevali).
- Arsenal won the PL and lost the UCL final to PSG on penalties. Salah left Liverpool. Gordon went Newcastle -> Barcelona for GBP69m. Cucurella went Chelsea -> Real Madrid for ~EUR50-60m. Konate and Bernardo Silva joined Real on frees. Atletico rejected Real's EUR150m bid for Julian Alvarez.

## 767460 Marmoush (conditional on leaving City; loans count)
- Joined City Jan 2025 for ~GBP59m; contract to 2029; wages ~GBP295k/wk; 8 PL starts in 2025-26.
- City open to offers (~EUR60-70m / GBP55m) - Flashscore Jun 1, CaughtOffside May 15.
- Barcelona "strong candidate" (Lewandowski replacement) - CaughtOffside May 15. Villa and Spurs interested (Football Insider); Juventus exploring (Tuttosport via Football Faithful, Jun 22).
- Galatasaray: first target if Icardi leaves, loan with purchase option (haberler.com, Jun 24).
- Player is open to a Bundesliga return.

## 974146 Fabinho (permanent; free agent)
- Al-Ittihad contract ended June 2026; renewal offered on lower terms was refused.
- Bolavip (Jun 21): his priority is a return to Europe, a Brazil return is not in plans, and he'll decide after the World Cup.
- Fenerbahce prioritising him (ASpor via YSscores, May 30), and an unnamed American club is interested. Palmeiras made a January proposal; Fluminense also interested.

## 222955 Bouaddi (Lille; non-conditional)
- 18 years old, Morocco international; contract to 2029. Lille president Letang: EUR50m is "not enough". Asking price is ~EUR70m (some say EUR80m).
- Arsenal "working on signing" him, with talks scheduled after the WC (Goal Jun 14 / The Times). TEAMtalk (Jun 24) says Arsenal lead.
- Liverpool held talks and sent officials to watch him. Real Madrid enquired (AS), but AS (Jun 23) says Mourinho thinks he is too young and Real are now prioritising Enzo.
- PSG are interested but focused on other positions (Romano). Chelsea, Bayern, Spurs and Man Utd are tracking.
- Unverified claim (cryptobriefing): Arsenal's EUR60m bid was rejected.

## 388162 Allan Elias (Palmeiras)
- 22-year-old winger, contract to Dec 2029. Palmeiras want ~EUR40m.
- Napoli offered EUR35m+5m in January (rejected) and their interest has cooled.
- Roma monitoring at ~EUR25m (Nicola, May 27). Newcastle and Zenit are now monitoring (palmeirasonline Jun 23).
- He rejected Inter Miami and wants Europe.

## 623398 Hadjam (Young Boys)
- Como opened talks on Jun 16-17 (Romano) as a fallback for Kaiki (Cruzeiro).
- Como then agreed the Kaiki deal (EUR2m loan + EUR14m obligation) on Jun 19-20 (ESPN Brasil, Gazzetta, ComoZero). That likely lowers Hadjam's Como chances, though Como are in the UCL and Alberto Moreno is leaving.
- Besiktas: tried in summer 2024 and Jan 2026 (wages stalled it) and planned to return in summer 2026 (star.com.tr May 18).
- Porto showed interest in a past window. He declined Spartak and prefers a top-5 league.
- Had ankle surgery in Jan 2026. Now at the WC with Algeria. New agency is DWMA.

## 588637 Nicolas Jackson (permanent from Chelsea)
- Bayern won't trigger the EUR65m option (Eberl, Apr 26). Chelsea contract runs to 2033.
- Juventus is the most active suitor. He was discussed in the Cambiaso swap talks (Gazzetta Jun 17, TEAMtalk Jun 19), but Juve prefer a loan with option (a loan would NOT count).
- Napoli, Newcastle, Fenerbahce and AC Milan are also linked.
- Football Insider (Jun 21): he will try to impress Alonso first, so any exit will come later in the window.

## 162316 Barcola (conditional on leaving PSG permanently)
- Asked to leave (TEAMtalk Jun 13); contract talks are on standby (Romano). Contract to 2028.
- Arsenal preparing a ~EUR80m bid, but PSG want ~EUR100m. Arsenal "ahead" per Football Insider Jun 23.
- Liverpool: Diomande is their priority and Barcola the backup, though they made a fresh attempt (Le10 via Mirror Jun 23).
- Bayern unlikely (Saibari). Many others have held talks (Man City, Man Utd, Chelsea, Barcelona, Real Madrid, Atletico, Inter, Napoli).

## 450664 Emiliano Martinez (conditional)
- Personal terms agreed with Juventus on a three-year deal (Corriere dello Sport via BBC Jun 11; Di Marzio). He is house-hunting in Turin.
- Villa are ready to sell (~EUR10m asked). They are lining up Zion Suzuki and Trafford as replacements, and Romano (Jun 10) said nothing was agreed club-to-club yet.
- Di Marzio (Jun 19): cost could make Juve withdraw, with Vicario as the alternative. Inter are long-term admirers.

## 876325 Enzo Fernandez (conditional)
- Agreement in principle with Real Madrid on a contract to 2032 (Schira, Jun 18).
- Real decided to go for him, and talks with Chelsea open in the coming days (AS Jun 23; BBC gossip Jun 24).
- Chelsea want ~GBP120m. Man City and PSG distanced themselves (BBC Jun 15).

## 20480 Martinelli (void if no transfer by Sep 30)
- Arsenal will sell (~GBP40-50m). Contract runs to 2027 plus an option. The Times says he is keen to explore a move abroad.
- Bayern enquired (most concrete per Flashscore), but Falk says they are unlikely to bid.
- PSG keen, with a possible swap angle involving Barcola. Atletico Madrid interested.

## 127253 Gabriel Jesus
- Arsenal lowered his price to GBP20-25m (CaughtOffside Jun 24). Contract to 2027.
- He prioritises England (British citizenship pending) and is not pushing to leave.
- Everton have a long-standing interest but no bid yet. Newcastle also linked.
- Juventus and Milan interested, but his GBP265k/wk wages are a problem. Palmeiras and Flamengo are sounding him out. He was left out of Brazil's WC squad.

## 875467 Highest PL fee of the window
- Elliot Anderson to Man City: the GBP106m+15m bid was rejected. Talks are advanced, Forest want more than the GBP125m British record, and the player wants City (talkSPORT Jun 24). Formalisation may wait until July for Forest's accounts.
- Diomande to Liverpool: the EUR90m+10m bid was rejected. Leipzig want EUR130m (~GBP112m), and Romano says ~EUR120m could get it done.
- Others: Rogers (Arsenal, ~GBP100m, Villa unwilling), Tonali (Spurs GBP85m+15m), Wharton (Liverpool, GBP70-100m), Barcola (EUR80-100m). Mateus Fernandes prefers Spurs.
