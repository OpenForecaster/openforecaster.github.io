- The US-Israel war on Iran began Feb 28 and killed Ali Khamenei; Mojtaba Khamenei became Supreme Leader on Mar 8.
- First ceasefire ~Apr 8 (Pakistan-brokered); Islamabad talks followed.
- US naval blockade of Iranian ports from mid-April. Operation Project Freedom (US minesweeping/escort) ran May 4-6.
- Early June: Israel-Iran flare-up (Israel hit Beirut, Iran fired missiles at Israel). The Houthis joined on Jun 8 with a ban on Israeli shipping and hit 2 ships in the Gulf of Aden on Jun 8-9.
- Jun 18: "Islamabad MOU", a US-Iran framework signed in Switzerland:
  - 60-day talks (to about Aug 17).
  - Hormuz reopens toll-free for 60 days; Iran is to clear mines within 30 days (Art. 5).
  - Iran is to work out Hormuz administration with Oman.
  - US blockade lifted; Treasury oil waiver issued Jun 22 for 60 days (through about Aug 21); assets unfrozen.
- Senate passed a war powers resolution 50-48 on Jun 23. Rubio toured the Gulf (UAE, Kuwait, Bahrain/GCC) Jun 23-25.
- Lebanon ceasefire is "largely holding"; Israel refuses to fully withdraw.
