## KEY LESSONS (for future forecasting sims)
1. Parallel research agents (19) + local article corpus = fast world-model building. Re-scan corpus DAILY for state changes.
2. Favorites cap: never exceed ~0.85-0.90; this sim world produced constant upsets (Vindman 0.95 lost; Trump endorsements failed: Ogles, Mills lost; heavy tennis/golf favorites fell).
3. "Question-designed events" (announced attempts like Kerr's mile record): weight the attempt outcome much higher (~0.45).
4. Vote/resolution dates sometimes precede criteria dates — resolution date in market.csv is authoritative.
5. Include ALL named entities from source articles (KCL; named officials lists).
6. POT/awards go to top statistical performers (Mooney) or champion's best player (Rodri); include defensive stars for Golden Ball-type awards.
7. Pole ≠ win (Leclerc British GP); sprint+pole ≠ win either.
8. Transfer surprises happen: always include 1-2 wildcard clubs (76ers took BOTH Brown and LeBron; Colo-Colo; Trabzonspor twice; Barcelona for Jesus; NextVision).
9. Escalating-conflict questions: follow the live escalation chain (Kyiv strikes, Iraq strikes) rather than static priors.
10. When a conditional question's condition may not occur, leave implicit mass for "void" (e.g., China football semi resolution).
