# Forecasting lessons from resumed sessions

- Verify event eligibility early: individual swimming distances differ from national team selection; a former minister may have resigned parliament before the target immunity window.
- Search the underlying event and investigations for fresh candidate names. Repeated searches containing only an old shortlist reinforce omissions. In Hungarian corruption cases include both hűtlen kezelés and sikkasztás while retaining their legal differences in resolution notes.
- Treat full candidate lists, partial narrative examples and nonexhaustive agenda excerpts differently. Absence from a short report is not proof of ineligibility.
- Read complete bookmaker boards, including longshots, before removing margins. Keep omitted-field mass under the five-outcome cap; mirrored feeds are correlated.
- Match aliases semantically in local diagnostic scoring: Vincent Brown/Bajinya, The Left/Die Linke, Oil and gas/Oil and gas extraction. These diagnostics are not official harness scores.
- Distinguish an official request from completed action, a projected lineup from a confirmed XI, and an announced signing from a completed numbered signing.
- Use each new athletic result once. Adjacent events offer modest readiness evidence; a medal in one distance does not settle another distance.
- One realized miss does not justify mechanically shrinking unrelated forecasts. Use the miss to improve candidate discovery and assess field uncertainty question by question.

- For questions asking who leads an interim sports table, fix completed results before pricing future matches. After Belgium2-0Italy and France1-0Turkey, the September28 leader is France only if it beats Belgium; Belgium also leads with a draw. Italy/Turkey cannot catch either winner or two four-point teams.
- A page dated on match day can still contain a preview written before the result or injury. A predicted Mbappe start after his confirmed squad departure is evidence the price/lineup context is stale. Treat it as a prior benchmark and use the confirmed later fact.
- Federation evaluation preferences, anonymous expectations, public endorsements and actual votes are different evidence stages. The number of votes held by favorable evaluators is not a pledged-vote count.

- Yuki Kurihara and Yuuki Kurihara denote the same Puyo Puyo player. A literal mismatch gives a false negative diagnostic; preserve both the raw spelling result and alias-aware local diagnostic. Store established equivalents by qid in known_forecast_aliases.json.

- Read the body of an appointment scoop and compare later same-day coverage. An anonymous expectation of a royal reception is a precursor, even if the headline says the appointment happens today. Check party eligibility before adding a floated Moroccan prime minister candidate.
- A previous court denial and a planned appeal change the prospective court distribution; neither is a stay granted on the target day. Distinguish verified reporting from actually retrieving the primary order, and preserve uncertainty about unexamined legal grounds.
- When all remaining outcomes fit the cap after a tournament advances, represent the closed survivor field directly. Remove eliminated competitors; do not leave a generic residual for a player who can no longer win.

- Ng Eain Yow and Eain Yow Ng are the same Malaysian squash player. Check given-name/family-name order before labeling a multiword athlete-name mismatch as a miss; preserve actual submitted spelling in forecast receipts.

- Rumesh Tharanga Pathirage and Rumesh Pathirage denote the same Sri Lankan javelin athlete; omission of a middle name does not make a different outcome. Add established equivalents before running the local reconciliation diagnostic.

- El Mansouri is the accepted surname-only form of Fatima Ezzahra El Mansouri, not an unnamed new appointee. Apply established full-name/surname aliases before judging a local resolution diagnostic.

- Convert three-way 90-minute football prices to knockout advancement before forecasting an eliminator. Normalize all three prices to remove margin, then add the draw probability times the favorite's conditional chance of winning extra time/penalties. Do not treat a quoted draw as a chance nobody advances.
- Docket search excerpts may show only the application and omit later entries. Independently dated contemporaneous denial reports can establish a decision while the extracted primary chunk remains incomplete; state clearly whether the actual order was retrieved.

- Pike closing review: a Supreme Court denial in one case did not close a separate Sixth Circuit proceeding. A short day-of interim stay satisfied the question even though later Supreme Court action could allow execution. The95% NoCourt forecast underweighted parallel litigation and temporary orders; search/case tracking must distinguish each procedural route before making an exhaustion-like probability assessment.
- For a question about a stay being granted, record the grant court and timestamp separately from later vacatur, final merits and actual execution. Disposition of one appeal is not disposition of every possible stay request.
